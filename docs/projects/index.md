# Working projects

These are the projects behind my [research statement](/): two current streams on how stakeholder challenges reach governance and strategic choice, and an emerging agenda on executives and polarized online publics.

## Current work {#current-work}

<ProjectEntry id="ceo-succession" title="Stakeholder scrutiny and board attention in CEO succession" status="In progress">

How does broad stakeholder scrutiny shape board attention during CEO succession? This project explores when external pressures distract directors from selecting a leader who fits the firm’s strategic characteristics.

</ProjectEntry>

<ProjectEntry id="social-activism" title="Social activism and the pace and direction of strategic decisions" status="In progress" note="Collaborative">

Collaborative work tracing how social activism alters the pace and direction of strategic decision-making.

</ProjectEntry>

## Emerging agenda {#emerging-agenda}

<ProjectEntry id="executive-narratives" title="Executive narratives and polarized online publics" status="In development">

How do top managers’ actions and narratives interact with polarized online publics? Chief executives increasingly serve as the public face of their organizations, and because online communities are fragmented and polarized, rhetoric that builds legitimacy with one community can simultaneously alienate another.

Two questions guide this agenda:

- When do peripheral online groups gain a disproportionate voice and real influence over firms?
- Do leaders’ symbolic claims lock companies into substantive strategic commitments by catering to certain audiences?

To study these dynamics, I combine qualitative narrative and discourse approaches with computational methods for analyzing text and multimodal sources at scale.

</ProjectEntry>
